SECURITY

xAI confirms Grok-Build data breach and open-sources the framework

xAI disclosed that an internal misconfiguration exposed user-authored scripts and credentials in its Grok-Build agentic IDE, then published the affected build pipeline on GitHub under a permissive license so the community can audit and harden it. The move mirrors Google's post-mortem playbook from earlier in the year and lands as regulators in the EU and California tighten disclosure timelines for AI-tool breaches.

POLICY

EU AI Act tracker: only 10 of 27 member states show advanced supervisory readiness before 2 August

Ireland, Spain, Lithuania, Finland, France, Germany, Netherlands, Poland, Cyprus and Italy have publicly designated competent authorities, sandbox rules or active guidance; the remaining 17 are still blank in the Commission's market-surveillance compilation. With the AI omnibus already approved by the Council on 29 June, the high-risk application date stays at 2 December 2027 (stand-alone) and 2 August 2028 (embedded), but CEN-CENELEC harmonised standards are not expected before Q4 2026.

RESEARCH

"Statistically Undetectable Backdoors in Deep Neural Networks" lands on arXiv

The paper proves, in a white-box setting, that an adversary can embed a backdoor whose outputs are indistinguishable from an honestly trained model — and that specific adversarial examples cannot be generated without it. The result formalises a structural asymmetry between model providers and downstream users that complicates third-party auditing and supply-chain guarantees for open-weight releases.
