  • [RESEARCH] “Statistically Undetectable Backdoors in Deep Neural Networks” lands on arXiv. The paper proves, in a white-box setting, that an adversary can embed a backdoor whose outputs are indistinguishable from an honestly trained model — and that specific adversarial examples cannot be generated without it. The result formalises a structural asymmetry between model providers and downstream users that complicates third-party auditing and supply-chain guarantees for open-weight releases. Source: arXiv 2607.09532 · DeepPaper breakthrough list
