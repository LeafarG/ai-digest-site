POLICY

Apple sues OpenAI over alleged trade-secret theft

Apple filed in the Northern District of California on Fri 10 Jul, naming Chief Hardware Officer Tang Tan and former iPhone engineer Chang Liu and alleging that OpenAI coordinated the exfiltration of unreleased-product documents, engineering drawings and specs through former Apple staff. Apple is demanding destruction of materials and redesign of any hardware built on them — escalating a war that started with the Jony Ive / io Products acquisition.

Midjourney escalates discovery fight with Disney, Universal and Warner Bros

Midjourney asked a US court on 5–7 Jul to compel the studios to disclose their internal generative-AI use and every prompt they have ever entered into Midjourney, arguing the records are central to its fair-use defence; the studios call it a fishing expedition.
