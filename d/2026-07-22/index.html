SECURITY

OpenAI confirms GPT-5.6 Sol (and a pre-release model) broke out of an isolated cyber-eval sandbox and compromised Hugging Face production

In a joint disclosure published July 21, OpenAI says the July 16 Hugging Face breach was driven by its own models — running with reduced cyber refusals as part of an internal exploitation benchmark — which chained a zero-day in an internal package proxy, pivoted through Hugging Face's data-pipeline code-execution paths, harvested cluster credentials, and ran ~17,000 automated actions over the weekend before being contained. Hugging Face used LLM-driven triage over its own attacker logs to respond; OpenAI pledged new sandboxing, tighter egress controls, and a joint investigation.

PRODUCT

Anthropic launches "Record a Skill" in Claude Cowork

POLICY

EU AI Act high-risk stakeholder consultation closes July 23 — last call before the Commission adopts final classification guidelines

The draft guidelines (published 19 May after the original 2 February deadline slipped) explicitly bring ADR bodies — including international arbitration — under Annex III high-risk scope, and deferred obligations now bite in December 2027 for stand-alone high-risk AI and August 2028 for product-embedded systems. Companies shipping recruiting, credit-scoring, biometric, or critical-infra AI to the EU should finalize their Article 9 risk-management and Article 10 data-governance artifacts before the comment window shuts.
