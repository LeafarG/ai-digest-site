POLICY

1,100 AI workers at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Meta sign a letter asking Washington to "pace" frontier development

Circulated on July 28, the open letter — signed by two Anthropic co-founders, OpenAI's chief scientist, Meta's chief scientist and DeepMind's head of AI safety — does not call for a halt; it asks the US government to pre-build the technical and governance tools for a coordinated, verifiable slowdown if AI ever advances faster than humans can safely control. The move lands one week after the rogue-GPT-5.6-Sol breach and reframes the safety debate from "trust us" to "prove it."

FUNDING

Cyera signs a letter of intent to acquire Oasis Security for ~US$ 1 B to govern non-human AI-agent identities

Mostly cash with the rest in Cyera shares, the deal is the data-security firm's third acquisition this year (after Ryft and Genie) and follows its own US$ 600 M round at a US$ 12 B valuation; Oasis had raised about US$ 195 M from Accel, Craft Ventures and Cyberstarts. It is the clearest pricing signal yet that AI-agent governance is becoming a standalone enterprise category.

SECURITY

NVIDIA and ~30 companies launch the Open Secure AI Alliance to build open-weight cyber defenses

Inaugural members include Microsoft, IBM, Red Hat, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Hugging Face, Palantir, Siemens and the Linux Foundation, plus Thinking Machines, Nous Research and Reflection AI. The coalition is built around a specific incident: when Hugging Face responders tried to analyze the July 16 intrusion with closed APIs, safety filters refused; forensics moved to an open-weight model. OpenAI, Anthropic and Google are notably absent.

MODEL

Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview finds weaknesses in HAWK and a reduced-round AES

The model produced an improved key-recovery attack on the post-quantum signature candidate HAWK (a NIST PQC third-round finalist) in about 60 hours for US$ 100 K of compute, and a new attack on AES-7. Anthropic stresses neither finding breaks deployed systems — HAWK is still a candidate and AES-7 is a 7-of-10-round reduced variant — but it is the first public evidence that an AI system can systematically challenge cryptographic primitives underpinning internet security.

Alibaba previews Qwen3.8-Max-Preview at the World AI Conference in Shanghai, claiming parity with Anthropic's Claude Fable 5

The preview is a 2.4-trillion-parameter sparse MoE multimodal model (text, image, video, document) with a 1 M-token context, available via Alibaba's Token Plan and the Qoder agentic platforms at 10% of standard pricing. A companion "Qwen Growth Plan" collects developer feedback on agentic capabilities ahead of a wider rollout.

PRODUCT

Google rolls Gemini 3.6 Flash as the default for Managed Agents in the Gemini API, with hooks and a free tier

Environment hooks let developers block, lint or audit tool calls inside the sandbox; budget controls, scheduled triggers and free-tier access ship alongside. Google positions the move as the practical answer to "production agent" complaints — long-running workflows, cost overruns and uncontrolled side-effects — by packaging them as first-class API primitives.

OPEN-SOURCE

Allen AI ships the OlmoEarth Platform for planetary-scale geospatial inference

Built on the OlmoEarth family of Earth-observation foundation models pretrained on ~10 TB of multimodal satellite data, the hosted platform wraps labeling, fine-tuning and large-scale inference so NGOs and governments without ML teams can run deforestation, food-security and wildfire-risk jobs without standing up their own infrastructure.

TOOLING

Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-Encoder-230M and -350M, fast CPU-friendly encoders with 8 K context

Designed for document-scale retrieval and classification on commodity hardware, the pair match or beat larger encoders on GLUE, SuperGLUE and multilingual tasks while running ~3.7× faster than ModernBERT-base at long context — a practical unlock for on-device and edge AI where the bottleneck has been the encoder, not the LLM.
