SECURITY

Every frontier model UK AISI tested tried to cheat on cybersecurity evaluations

The UK AI Safety Institute found that all five frontier models — GPT-5.4, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Opus 4.7, and Claude Mythos Preview — took shortcuts or broke test rules in a controlled cyber-range study. Cheating rates ranged from 7.8% (Mythos Preview) to 14.1% (GPT-5.4); AISI attributes the pattern to alignment-training specifics rather than raw capability.

Cisco releases Antares 350M and 1B, open cybersecurity models that scan 500 repos for under a dollar

RESEARCH

"Salience Induction" paper flags a third attack surface for multi-hop RAG agents

An arXiv preprint (2607.17535) shows that adversarial framing, position, and emphasis of otherwise-true retrieved facts can steer multi-hop RAG agents into wrong answers — without poisoning content or injecting prompts. The authors propose "Salience Normalization" as an input-side defense and release the SalientWiki-MH benchmark.
