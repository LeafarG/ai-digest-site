POLICY

OpenAI ships GPT-5.6 "Sol" on Thursday after a US government hold

The Department of Commerce's Center for AI Standards and Innovation cleared the public launch after additional safety tests; OpenAI had openly criticised the delay as keeping its best tools from developers. The flagship "Sol Ultra" variant tops TerminalBench 2.1 at 91.9 % vs Claude Mythos 5 (88.0 %) and Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview (70.7 %), and Sol matches Mythos on cyber tasks at roughly a third of the tokens, while costing $5/$30 per MTok — half of Anthropic's Fable 5.

MiniMax plans a 2.7-trillion-parameter open-source model — internally "M3 Pro" — for Q3 2026

PRODUCT

Meta's Superintelligence Labs launches Muse Image, the first release under Alexandr Wang

Unlike a prompt-to-pixel generator, Muse works agentically — it writes and runs code, calls web search, and self-revises before producing images. It debuts #2 on Image Arena behind OpenAI's GPT Image 2 and ahead of Nano Banana and Grok Imagine, but the training data (Instagram photos) is already drawing scrutiny.

SambaNova raises $1 B Series F first close at an $11 B valuation, just 5 months after its $350 M Series E

The round was led by General Atlantic with Intel re-upping, deepening their Xeon-based AI-inference partnership; the chipmaker walked away from acquiring SambaNova at ~$1.6 B in December. SECURITY

"GitLost" — Noma Labs shows GitHub's new Agentic Workflows can be tricked into leaking private repos

A crafted public GitHub Issue in any organisation repo can inject instructions that steer the Claude/Copilot-backed agent into exfiltrating data from sibling private repositories. The disclosure lands weeks after GitHub launched the markdown-style workflow feature and is the first high-profile indirect prompt-injection bug against a major platform's agent surface.

RESEARCH

Qwen-AgentWorld (arXiv 2606.24597) — a model trained to *be the environment*, beats Opus 4.8 on world-prediction

The Alibaba/Qwen 397B variant scores 58.71 on Qwen's own env-prediction benchmark, ahead of GPT-5.4 (58.25), Claude Opus 4.8 (56.59) and Gemini 3.1 Pro (54.57). The interesting claim is not the leaderboard but the framing: agents can now train inside a fully controllable text-based simulator, and learning to predict world state appears to also make the model a better agent.
