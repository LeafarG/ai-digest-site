AMD commits up to US$5B to Anthropic for 2 GW of Instinct MI450 GPUs

Announced late on the 22nd and confirmed through the 23rd, AMD will take a strategic stake of up to $5B in Anthropic while Anthropic commits to deploying up to 2 gigawatts of MI450 accelerators in AMD's Helios rack-scale system — first gigawatt in H1 2027 — with broad ROCm engineering collaboration and Claude adoption inside AMD. The deal joins Anthropic's existing infrastructure stack with Google, Broadcom, Amazon, SpaceX, and TeraWulf and reorders the GPU supplier duopoly narrative.

POLICY

Federal judge grants final approval to Anthropic's US$1.5B copyright settlement

U.S. District Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín signed off on the Bartz v. Anthropic class action on the 20th (republished widely on the 22nd-23rd), the largest copyright class action settlement in U.S. history at roughly $3,000 per work across an estimated 500,000 works. The settlement closes the case but does not establish appellate precedent: training on purchased text was ruled fair use, while the pirate-library acquisition path remains a per-case question still in litigation against Google, Meta, Midjourney, and OpenAI.
