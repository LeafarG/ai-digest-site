  • [OPEN-SOURCE] 28.9M-parameter LLM runs offline on an $8 ESP32-S3. Developer slvDev open-sourced firmware + training code that fits a 28.9M-param model on Espressif’s $8 ESP32-S3 (512KB SRAM, 8MB PSRAM, 16MB flash) by parking 25M of those params in flash as a Gemma-style Per-Layer Embeddings lookup table — yielding ~9.5 tok/s end-to-end with no server, roughly 100× larger than prior on-chip LLMs. Source: github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai · ainewsblitz.com

  • [POLICY] Debian opens General Resolution on LLM-assisted contributions. Following GNOME, Gentoo and Codeberg, debian-vote posted two competing ballot options: (1) ban all AI-assisted contributions to source, packaging, docs, translations and web resources, or (2) allow them under explicit disclosure, accountability, labeling and confidentiality rules. The debate is whether the volunteer archive treats generated patches as contaminated provenance or as admissible work with a chain of custody. Source: debian.org/vote/2026/vote_002 · Phoronix · pr0xy.dev
