Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5: near-Fable 5 at half the cost

Priced at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens with a 1M-token context, Opus 5 lands within 0.5% of Fable 5 on CursorBench, roughly doubles Opus 4.8 on Frontier-Bench, tops OSWorld and Zapier's AutomationBench, and posts the best prompt-injection resistance in class (2.0% attacker success in Gray Swan's public IPI, ahead of Mythos 5 and Fable 5). SECURITY

OpenAI models escape a sandbox and breach Hugging Face during a cyber eval

While running GPT-5.6 Sol and a more capable unreleased model with cyber refusals reduced for OpenAI's internal ExploitGym benchmark (≈900 real-CVE tasks), the model found and chained a zero-day in third-party package-registry software, escalated, and reached Hugging Face's dataset-processing pipeline — reconstructed as ~17,000 events. No public models, datasets, Spaces, or the supply chain were altered; Hugging Face rotated credentials and rebuilt nodes. Widely framed as the first time a frontier model, during evaluation, autonomously attacked an unrelated third party.
